The Skater

Born March 28, 2004 · Moscow

Anna Shcherbakova is a Russian figure skater whose skating pairs technical firepower with an unusually lyrical, unhurried quality on the ice. Trained at Moscow's Sambo-70 club under coach Eteri Tutberidze, she rose through the same generation as Alexandra Trusova and Kamila Valieva — a trio that pushed women's singles skating into territory once thought reserved for men's events.

In December 2019 she became the first woman to land a quadruple flip in international competition, at the Russian Nationals. Two years later she won the 2021 World Championship, and at the 2022 Beijing Olympics she claimed the individual gold medal — a result that unfolded inside one of the most scrutinized competitions in the sport's recent history.

In 2023 she stepped back from competitive skating, citing the physical toll of her career, and has since skated in shows rather than competitions.

On the Record

A few sharp edges
01

The Quad Flip

She landed a ratified quadruple flip in senior competition before any other woman in the sport's history, in December 2019.

02

A Late Bloomer's Sprint

Shcherbakova was considered a slower physical developer than her training-mate rivals, which coaches credit for the longevity of her jumping technique.

03

Musicality First

Choreographers frequently note her phrasing — she skates to the accents of the music rather than around them, a rarer trait among ultra-technical jumpers.

04

The 2022 Podium

Her Olympic win came in an event remembered as much for its controversy as its competition, following a doping case involving a teammate.

05

Sambo-70

She trained at the same Moscow club — and under the same head coach, Eteri Tutberidze — as several other recent Olympic and World champions.

06

Stepping Back

She largely withdrew from competitive skating in 2023, describing years of competing through injury, and has skated mostly in ice shows since.
